2. WELCOME

Welcome to Greenfield College! Our campus provides a friendly environment for learning, developing skills, and participating in student activities. This handbook covers basic campus information and student guidelines.

3. ACADEMIC GUIDELINES

- Classes run Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM.
- Students must maintain at least 75% attendance.
- Submit assignments through the student portal before the deadline.
- Internal assessments take place twice each semester.
- Examination schedules are published 15 days before exams.
- For academic support, contact your faculty advisor, Dr. Ananya Sharma.

5. STUDENT CONDUCT

- Carry your college ID card while on campus.
- Treat students, faculty, and staff respectfully.
- Keep classrooms and shared spaces clean.
- Obtain permission before using restricted facilities.
- Cheating and plagiarism may lead to disciplinary action.
- Bullying, harassment, and ragging are prohibited.
- Smoking and alcohol consumption are prohibited on campus.

6. LIBRARY AND LAB RULES

- Students may borrow up to three books for 14 days.
- Renew books before the due date, subject to availability.
- Keep phones on silent in the library.
- Food and drinks are not allowed in computer labs.
- Install software only with lab administrator approval.
- Report damaged equipment to the lab assistant immediately.

7. CAMPUS SAFETY

- Security staff are available at the main gate 24 hours a day.
- The emergency assembly point is the main sports ground.
- Follow marked emergency exits during an evacuation.
- Report lost belongings to the security desk.
- Report accidents or unsafe conditions to the nearest staff member.
